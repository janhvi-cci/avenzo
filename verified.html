Trust Architecture

Trust Before Business.

AVENZO Verified is a structured business verification framework designed to build confidence across the EV ecosystem by assessing credibility, capability and readiness.

The Triad of Trust

Three Pillars of Business Verification

Creating a robust trust layer that accelerates conversion at every stage of the network.

1. Credibility

Understanding the business behind the profile. Assessing corporate registration, entity history, ownership structure and operating track record.

✓ Verified Corporate Entity & KYC

2. Capability

Understanding what the organisation can realistically deliver. Evaluating manufacturing scale, quality systems, testing standards and homologations.

✓ Production Capacity & Testing Standards

3. Readiness

Understanding whether the organisation is prepared for commercial engagement. Verifying supply chain dependability, warranty backing and after-sales service.

✓ Warranty SLA & Field Support
Comprehensive Review

The 7 Assessment Dimensions

A structured review process covering critical operational, technical, and commercial criteria.

01

Company Background

Registration, corporate ownership structure, business history and operational track record.

02

Manufacturing Capability

Production capacity, tooling, quality management systems (ISO), and component localization.

03

Product Information

Full technical specifications, battery chemistry, real-world range/payload data, and test reports.

04

Certifications & Compliance

Statutory approvals, ARAI / ICAT type approvals, AIS-156 safety compliance and CMVR certification.

05

Warranty Commitments

Coverage scope, battery lifecycle warranty terms, escalation pathways, and claim turnaround times.

06

Service & Spare Parts

Service network readiness, spare parts availability, technician training programs and logistics SLAs.

07

Production & Delivery

Actual monthly capacity, confirmed order lead times and supply chain dependability indicators.
