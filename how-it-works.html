End-to-End Journey

The 6-Stage Engagement Framework

Each stage is designed to add measurable value and reduce commercial risk before the next begins.

01

Join AVENZO

Submit your organisation profile, product categories, operating states, and strategic objectives through the native registration flow.

02

Share Business Profile

Provide verified operational data: manufacturing scale, homologation certificates, dealer infrastructure, or fleet deployment requirements.

03

Review & Verification

AVENZO conducts structured assessment across credibility, capability and readiness dimensions, creating a trusted baseline.

04

Opportunity Identification

Algorithmically and curatorially identify matching dealership requests, institutional RFQs, or fleet procurement pipelines.

05

Business Connection

Direct, pre-vetted commercial introduction with documentation, eliminating cold outreach and unverified catalog browsing.

06

Partnership & Growth

Integrate necessary enablers (charging infra, debt financing, warranty servicing) to scale durable, repeat business.

Stakeholder Paths

Tailored Journeys Across the Ecosystem

How each participant uses AVENZO to accomplish high-priority commercial goals.

For EV Manufacturers

Expand distribution channels rapidly without building regional field sales teams from scratch.

  • Match with financially stable regional distributors and multi-brand dealers.
  • Gain visibility into large-volume corporate fleet RFQs and institutional tenders.
  • Present verified credentials and AIS homologation data directly to qualified buyers.

For Distributors & Dealers

De-risk dealership investments by connecting with verified OEMs that have proven manufacturing capacity.

  • Access pre-vetted EV manufacturers with clear product specs and testing proof.
  • Secure regional territory clarity and transparent warranty backing.
  • Connect with local after-sales service and spare parts networks.

For Fleets & Businesses

Procure commercial EV fleets with integrated financing, charging and maintenance bundles.

  • Run structured RFQs matching specific payload, range and duty cycle requirements.
  • Connect with leasing partners and NBFCs offering competitive commercial terms.
  • Bundle turnkey charging infrastructure and maintenance SLA contracts.

For Institutions

Fulfill statutory zero-emission mandates with end-to-end institutional support.

  • Structured procurement pathways for universities, PSUs, hospitals and municipalities.
  • Neutral coordination layer to assess supplier compliance and statutory testing.
  • Turnkey integration of charging infrastructure and long-term upkeep.
