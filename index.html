Strategic Roadmap

One Network. PAN-India Reach.

Moving methodically from a high-quality focused pilot to India's comprehensive national EV business infrastructure.

Phase 01

Focused Pilot

Establishing baseline density and proven conversion in primary automotive corridors.

  • 10–15 credible manufacturers
  • 20–30 selected products
  • 50–100 distributors/dealers
  • Selected fleet/business buyers
  • 3–5 initial states
Phase 02

Regional Scale

Expanding proven vehicle categories and replicating across adjacent regional clusters.

  • More manufacturers
  • Expanded distributor reach
  • More fleet & business demand
  • Integrated charging & finance
Phase 03

PAN-India Network

Connecting regional EV supply with demand across northern, western, southern and eastern corridors.

  • National distributor network
  • Multi-region bulk RFQs
  • Institutional opportunities
  • Full ecosystem participation
Phase 04

National Infrastructure

The trusted national network connecting India's EV businesses, products, services and opportunities.

  • End-to-end B2B connectivity
  • Standardized verification
  • Institutional integration
