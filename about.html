Mission & Strategy

Building the Business Infrastructure Behind India's EV Transition

India's EV market is scaling rapidly, but commercial fragmentation slows deployment. AVENZO is the structured B2B network connecting supply, distribution, institutional demand, and enablers.

Why AVENZO Exists

The Missing Layer in Indian Electric Mobility

The opportunity is no longer only about selling vehicles. India's EV ecosystem now requires coordinated networks across manufacturing, distribution, financing, charging, service, spare parts, fleet deployment and compliance.

The next phase of EV growth depends not only on vehicles, but on the networks that move them into real businesses. The EV value chain is expanding faster than the business infrastructure connecting its participants. AVENZO aims to be that infrastructure.

Core Message:
"India has EV supply. India has EV demand. The missing layer is trusted business connectivity."

Segments Driving Ecosystem Complexity

Electric 2-Wheelers

Largest volume segment; dense urban and semi-urban distribution requirements.

Electric 3-Wheelers

Last-mile logistics and passenger mobility; high institutional procurement potential.

Commercial & Fleet EVs

Corporates, logistics operators, government fleets; bulk procurement with service requirements.

Institutional Adoption

Universities, hospitals, PSUs, and municipalities moving actively to EV mandates.

Macro Alignment

Government Policy Creates the Environment — AVENZO Connects the Ecosystem

Accelerating the operational impact of key national policy frameworks including PM E-DRIVE and PLI-Auto.

PM E-DRIVE Framework

Demand Signals & Charging Infrastructure

Supports EV adoption across commercial vehicle categories, public charging networks and testing standards. AVENZO helps businesses access eligible vehicle categories with structured procurement pathways.

PLI-Auto Scheme

Advanced Automotive Technology & Localization

Drives domestic manufacturing scale-up, zero-emission automotive components and progressive supply chain localization. AVENZO connects these manufacturers directly to nationwide distribution channels.

Long-Term Vision

Build Focused. Prove Value. Expand Across India.

Our strategic roadmap for establishing India's national EV business network infrastructure.

Phase 01

Focused Pilot

High-density validation across select initial states and target commercial segments.

  • 10–15 credible manufacturers
  • 20–30 selected products
  • 50–100 distributors/dealers
  • Selected fleet/business buyers
  • 3–5 initial states
Phase 02

Regional Scale

Expand successful categories and operating regions into adjacent manufacturing hubs.

  • More manufacturers
  • Expanded distributor reach
  • More fleet & business demand
  • Integrated charging & finance
Phase 03

PAN-India Network

Connect regional EV supply with demand across northern, southern, western & eastern corridors.

  • National distributor network
  • Multi-region RFQs
  • Institutional procurement
  • Broad ecosystem integration
Phase 04

National Infrastructure

The trusted national network connecting India's EV businesses, products, services and opportunities.

  • End-to-end B2B connectivity
  • Standardized verification
  • National coordination
